ER MODEL — IMPROVEMENTS FROM THE PRELIMINARY DESIGN

The revised ER model clarifies the relationships, key constraints,
and cardinalities from the preliminary design. The LANE entity
uses a composite primary key consisting of origin_hub_id and
destination_hub_id, preserving the direction of each route.
The VEHICLE specialization represents BOX_TRUCK,
REFRIGERATED_VAN, and RAIL_CAR with their type-specific
attributes, while common vehicle relationships remain attached
to the VEHICLE superclass. In particular, BasedAt connects
VEHICLE to HUB, and Runs connects VEHICLE to DEPARTURE.
TravelsOn connects DEPARTURE to LANE. The revised diagram also
clarifies mandatory and optional participation in relationships
and maintains Region as an attribute of HUB rather than a
separate entity. The final ER model follows the zyBooks
conceptual notation and reflects the implemented relational
schema and business constraints.
